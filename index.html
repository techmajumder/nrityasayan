About

My Dance
Journey

I have been learning the Sabarno Dance style under Guru Sri Sabarnik De at Brahma-Kamal Institute of Dance and Drawing.

My practice explores classical Indian movement, expression, rhythm and contemporary interpretation, allowing different influences to come together through movement.

“Sabarno Dance style is a fusion of Natya Shastra, Uday Shankar style and Odissi.” Sabarno Dance Style

Foundations

The Influences
Behind Movement

My learning is shaped by multiple traditions and approaches to Indian movement, rhythm, expression and stage presence.

01 — FOUNDATION

Natya Shastra

Exploring the principles of Indian performance, expression, gesture, movement and dramatic communication.

02 — EXPRESSION

Uday Shankar

Discovering a modern approach to Indian dance through expressive movement, visual composition and physical storytelling.

03 — RHYTHM

Odissi

Learning from the grace, posture, rhythm and expressive vocabulary associated with the Odissi tradition.
