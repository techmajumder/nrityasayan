My Dance
Journey

Three years of learning, movement and discovering expression through dance.

I am learning the Sabarno Dance style under my Guru, Sri Sabarnik De , at Brahma-Kamal Institute of Dance and Drawing . My practice explores the relationship between movement, rhythm, expression and interpretation.

“Sabarno Dance style is a fusion of Natya Shastra, Uday Shankar style and Odissi.”

The philosophy

Movement becomes expression.
Rhythm becomes story.
