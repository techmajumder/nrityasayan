Journey

I have been learning the Sabarno Dance style under Guru Sri Sabarnik De at Brahma-Kamal Institute of Dance and Drawing for the past three years.

My practice explores the relationship between classical Indian movement, expression, rhythm and contemporary interpretation.

“Sabarno Dance style is a fusion of Natya Shastra, Uday Shankar style and Odissi.”
