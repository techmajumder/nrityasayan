01 · About

My Dance Journey

I have been learning the Sabarno Dance style under Guru Sri Sabarnik De at Brahma-Kamal Institute of Dance and Drawing.

My practice explores classical Indian movement, expression, rhythm and contemporary interpretation. Through dance, I continue to discover new ways of communicating emotion and storytelling.

"Sabarno Dance style is a fusion of Natya Shastra, Uday Shankar style and Odissi."
Philosophy
Movement becomes meaning when expression becomes honest.
